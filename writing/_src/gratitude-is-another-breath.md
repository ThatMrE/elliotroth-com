title: gratitude is another breath
date: 2024-12
shelf: poetry

There is nothing lonelier than a dial tone
There is nothing more sacred than to listen to the deep breathing as your lover falls asleep next to you
I feel the rubber band of us stretch across time zones
Across bruises and electrocutions and sunburns and it’s like breathing
I breathe you in each morning
And breathe you out at the end of each day
Holding my breath to try to keep your laugh inside me
Escaping out in my dreams
A full body smile
Thank you for being alive
Thank you for being alive
Thank you for being alive
It is so precious to spend time in this way
It is so meaningful to write these words
To speak these truths
To feel it all in my body
Breathless and wild we are
What dreams may come
