title: romance is the shape formed between two people

My definition of romance
has a lot to do with something I once learned from a sculptor
she said that you can treat the space between two people
like a piece of artwork
feel the contours of it like a living breathing thing
and lean into the shape of it
to understand when it is finally safe to fall asleep
because being in love is like falling asleep in class
slow then all of the sudden fast
tumbling and bumbling
I can’t romance you
it’s not a verb, it’s not something you do to someone else
that feels like an imposition
I can only undo the latch on my joy, my care, my pain
and let them all out to rest in the meadow
When you tell me cows have best friends
that’s romantic
when you share with me a movie that means a lot to you
when you walk outside to fart
that’s romantic
We create these moments between ourselves to remember that mean so very much
And the cows dance together in the meadow
and I would want nothing more than to dance with you my dear
so let’s lean on each other like heavyweight champions 16 rounds in
let’s fall without the splat
and catch in each other’s minds
my record skips and plays again
my record skips and plays again
my record skips and plays again
take me to bed already
