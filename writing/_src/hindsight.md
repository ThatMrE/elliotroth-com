[right]
Never understood how she could lie to herself so well
I try to sift through discoveries of my ominous omissions like
I’m drowning in quicksand
Advice: “Don’t worry, be happy”
a McFerrin throwback as I throw up
swirling down the toilet bowl is Truth
(we’re all running from something)
It flees the scene like its running from something
as a disapproving witness the fan clicks
Overhead
wagging its finger at me like an old hag
bitterness of self-loathing so sweet on my tongue
while hooligans, vagabonds
villainous vile vermin gnaw my skull
they chip away at the stone-cold countenance
chiseling
graphic profanity
into the recesses of my mind
driving home
Pounding Sledgehammers
Behind my eyes (she had green eyes)
I keep falling into the same pattern
the same cycle
year after year in dancing in life’s highway
SLAM! on the ground
SLAM! hit the floor
SLAM! almost drowned
SLAM! Always    Want    More
[spread] killing myself slowly                       softly                                            silently
I guess its good practice for later
White lies kill Black ideas
Racism for the colorblind
and my vision has never been 20/20

He likes to invent things
He could build a machine to stitch my soul back together
if He wanted to, he could fix me
we could be happy
at the end of the world
you could find me in Death Valley outrunning the sun
(we’re all running from something)
begging, please carry me away from my burns
I got them cooking breakfast the next morning
I’m in over my head
My mother fell in love with a Liar
I saw how their lives twisted, bent and broke
and I swore not to let my life crash like a wave
my body torn apart by seagulls on the beach
we are all just made of Water
and Water erodes
let yourself flow into me
but you are dammed
up to Barcroft
I’ve stopped caring if I drown
in your eyes (he had brown eyes)
I wonder if God sees the designs I scratched into your back
the drawn out roadmap of us
when I know one night is not enough to take me around the world
the graves they dig for couples
are dug one-by-one
each is buried alone in the ground
because the dead do not fear loneliness
quietly they let worms wiggle through their hearts
when you rested your glasses on my solitary nightstand
did you see how they fractured the light of the lamp
so that the broken colors of the rainbow spilled out like blood
and I was trapped in your prism
