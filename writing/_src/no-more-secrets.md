no: 71
title: no more secrets
date: 2024-12
shelf: poetry
book: litany
---

We used to tell the bees the important things
now we stumble over syllables
watching murmurations through mirrors
Soft stones break bones and it’s these
Leaded gifts which become worn with anticipation
wear with me in their anticipation
We both dabble around waiting
To bite, catch flight
To get where we mean
At the bottom of the world, the emperor penguin gives a smooth pebble memento
Scratched and cold, bought and sold
to carry the heart so that we skip in tempo
Who houses the home to hide what the mouth can’t fit
The flowers are blooming late this season
Each blossom aching for secrets
wild one, are you ever fully planted?
shame they say dandelions are a debasement
when the whispered truth behind ocean eyes is stormy amazement

The animals know when it’s about to rain
They grow quiet in anticipation
why place stones instead of flowers
life is but death’s hesitation

This pause before the break
shatter before the aches
it feels like deception
it’s a shelter from the wave
the shadow from the grave
Two joined at the reflection

Taste the echo and tell me
how bitter the rind in your teeth
the heart of a plum
juice dribbling down your chin
what are we but secrets that we cannot keep

Blood red auto-cannibalism
you only dance alone
