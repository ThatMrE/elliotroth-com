no: 98
title: fly
date: 2024-12
shelf: poetry
book: litany
---

hey baby bird
pushing towards the edge
clamoring crying me too
don’t jump yet
your feathers ain’t big enough
you’d fall and splat
hey baby bird
just hold tight
I’m sure mama is coming
I can tell you’re hungry
me too
I can tell you’re cold
me too
I can tell in this cramped nest
you’re itching to escape
me too me too
mama is coming
the world is bright and big
and can crush the life out of you
in one fell swoop
and I’m sitting here in the airport
watching eyes wide
making a nest of my bags and my life
tiny heart crying for change
me too
wings too small to fly away
I hope you can hold the weight of it all
the weight of you
that you grow strong
before the days feel heavy
me too me too
