title: ask softly
date: 2024-12
shelf: poetry
book: litany
---

Please excuse my broken bits
they fell like Legos on the floor
and I’ve been meaning to pick them up
but if you want
I’d like for you to stay awhile
and help me arrange the pieces
so they don’t hurt while walking
it goes a lot quicker with someone else
and we could build beautiful things

please
I didn’t mean to scare you off
where do the words go
when they die in your mouth
how do they taste
fighting down your throat
like an octopus
reaching for openness
choking out life

I feel like I’m undressing
on the beach in the wind
no towel
bruised bloody and wet
it’s so cold in your eyes
come closer and kiss me
so we can close our eyes
and see with our hands
I want to tell you about all my scars
I want to make up secrets
just so I can tell you them
I want
you
for the first time in a long time
I’m asking questions

I’ve started this ritual
a ceremony almost
every evening I pretend
I’m made of something new
