---
no: 51
title: Snow / Sun
date: 2015-11
shelf: poetry
book: allograft
---

soft static drifts
chill me to the bone
as your joy turns to ashes in your mouth
write me a religion
bury me in an old folks home
race a mountain
break the ice
dive deep and come up shivering

I’ll dig a fire
in the pits of your eyes
as the avalanche of your hair
smothers me
spit freezes on our lips
while I dig down
to find warmth
at the core
frostbitten and sore
I let your storm take me

Remember that time we went racing?
You and I, the sand kicking our backs
the wind biting our heels
we ran
You looked at me
and I looked at you
and I swear I saw us go supernova
in your gold eyes
we melted
and fell like droplets
on the face of the earth
crashing into the surf

When did we go?
when did we let time unravel
the womb-baskets of our bodies
like armor
we crumble
we break
let my limbs rust with you
until we can no longer start until each step
leaves pieces of us behind
footsteps in the sand
