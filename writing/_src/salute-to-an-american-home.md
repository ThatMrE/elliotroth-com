---
no: 62
title: Salute to an American Home
date: 2015-11
shelf: poetry
book: allograft
---

She was a sock without a pair
making a breakfast of leftovers
using burnt hands
to sculpt two lovers
like clay into hardtack
serving it raw
calling it
morning after medicine

I broke my teeth on her pieces
and choked on tattered remains
of an American flag
throwing it all up

freedom tastes like an empty home
