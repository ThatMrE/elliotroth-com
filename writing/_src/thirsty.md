Being with you feels like
biking with my eyes closed
like finally dropping the filters
during a funny sexy strip tease
like my favorite secret
(even though I want to tell everyone)

this is little kid stuff
sending notes during class
pretending to pay attention
while ouroborous of brains devoured
by your palm on my chest

maybe I’m not grown enough
maybe I don’t want to be
maybe all I want is to
let the storms of your pain drown me
until I learn to breathe salt water

There’s a ice blue eye in this hurricane
shot with lightning bolts of green
that send shivers through my body
when you hold my gaze

let me drink you in
I can be thirsty like the high desert
even when it hurts
calm
quiet
this horizon is endless
so that the flash flood river of your mind can snake through
and when we hold each other in the shower
we can lean against each other
weary like prizefighters
and watch the water
wash it all away

Sip my coffee
and I’ll sip your tea
and we’ll build a code language together
pillowforts during these sleepovers
try to catch summer in a bottle
race each other to the stop sign
and even though we’re both competitive
we’ll take turns letting the other win
