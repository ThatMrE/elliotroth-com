reading Ginsberg in a bookstore
because that’s what deep introspective people do dammit
It’s literary porn with slick fellatio dreams
more of the same shit I can see online
with less of a figurative flair
The minute you’ve seen Betty white fucking a horse
you’ve been desensitized to most of everything
the sun kissing my face doesn’t awaken me
and the weather is getting colder so I might as well stay in bed
Instead of fucking with my head
it’s a gonzo type of journalistic intrigue with
slavish mind bitches getting drained like batteries
powering the summer heat through winter
Giving everyone acid dreams
If this is the matrix, does it matter if it’s real or not?
if everything is swathed in latex and chrome
it still doesn’t feel like home
We’re just a bunch of cocaine cowboys
riding into the sunset on an electric cool whip zebra
