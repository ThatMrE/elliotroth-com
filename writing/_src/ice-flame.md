---
no: 69
title: Ice & Flame
date: 2015-11
shelf: poetry
book: allograft
---

I think everyone is a little scarred

you can’t help but understand pain
it has brought you to where you are
scorch marks like a map
curling up legs and down faces
like serpents

pain has brought me to where I am

my ears become feet
and I travel the train-track-stitches
scratches and pock-marked mountains
with my fingernails scrabbling for purchase in your earth
to avoid the heat

There are two kinds of people in this world

you and me we’re fire and ice
Prometheus and Persephone
charcoal and pomegranates
blood and stone
banished in each other

Chained to past mistakes, they forever take

majestic eagles tear at my insides
while you hide in your own Hades
all we ever wanted was spring warmth and comfort
now we know not to reach so high
moonshots die in the cold of space
and flame as they fall
We’re binary stars wrapped in self-made gravity of situations

Either freeze or burn
you have a choice
move like a glacier slowly carving
or flash fire quick all-consuming
both leaving scars on the land in their wake

Eventually all embers grow cold
Eventually all icebergs melt

Come end with me
