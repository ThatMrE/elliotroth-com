title: in time we all self immolate
date: 2024-12
shelf: poetry
book: litany
---

I crossed an ocean
and when I landed checked my map
Delighted at how I am further
and farther than I’ve ever been
There’s this drumbeat this loud sound
deep deep in me
thrumming
making me move
pushing me far
my engine
my freedom
my reason

my terror
raised up like a wave late at night
This is ending this is ending this is ending
the quiet comprehending
I would make my ex remove her watch before coming to bed
During shiva we cover mirrors
reset the clocks trying to save daylight
clocks are a type of mirror

I grew up held in difference
100 countries, 40 languages
a minority means where I feel myself
the edges of me into the edges of you
melting like chocolate fondue
frying like harissa carrot pomegranate pancakes
we ache for the taste

our Spanish immersion class learned math
the foreign syllables on our tongues
every year we’d have 100 day
each student bringing in 100 of
something to count in spanish
I brought paperclips
later
learning you could trade paperclips for a house
learning the world might end in paperclips
I wish I brought something different

I once listened to a friend, an astronaut
describe the feeling
of opening the cupola
and looking back at the planet
bathed in earthglow
feeling the edges of self melt away
flooded with love
overwhelmed into tears
pooling in their eyes
in the dark of space

I can feel time pull at me
tugging insistent like a child at my sleeve
pulling me towards something greater
away from a shadow
towards the light
both of them me
I’ll keep trying to send messages
through the ether
only receiving return to sender

There is a palace here built on the hill
That was cursed
and to lift the curse required blood
a willing volunteer
to be entombed inside
for all time
Is it a curse that I hear?
Is it tinnitis?
Is it the child chasing me?
Throwing paperclips at my heels
They built the palace by sliding blocks of stone on slabs of ice
and waiting for them to melt

I am a palace built of ice waiting to melt
to settle in to the right places
to entomb the past as sacrifice
trying to bask in earthlight
hearing the room full of clocks hidden away at the center
tick tick tick winding down
