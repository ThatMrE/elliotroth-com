title: i am but a mouth
date: 2024-12
shelf: poetry
book: litany

When they tell you it takes guts
believe them
life is lemons
you’re shaken not stirred so drink up
feel the twist in your stomach
that tastes like truth
it’s an unruly bitch
for the first time in your life
try not to disappear
this righteous anger that’s been stewing inside you
makes for a fortified broth
no need to swallow insults
when you get punched in the face
punch back
this is an all out brawl
a fist fight with good intentions
how foolish was it to suffer fools
keep it cool and take an eye
steal yourself for the gods
seek out the blind
and give them something to talk about
you no longer kiss, you’ve got bite
so when you tear into a frog
don’t let it catch in your throat instead
screaming because you are no longer a void
every night that drags on
let it be known that you’re collecting dog tags
because to be truly unleashed
you can’t have anything to bitch about
cry havoc and let loose
wash it away
no more tears
it’s everyone else who needs to fear
