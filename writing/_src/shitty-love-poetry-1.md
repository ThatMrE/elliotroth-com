Can I find religion in you?
In your hair, in your stare, in the little way you bite your lip
and how you always eat things by sticking out your tongue tip…
If I write you shitty love poetry is that a cliché? Am I “gay?”
If so then call me Liberachi, let me worship Neil Patrick Harris’s body
let’s get fucked up on Bicardi and move to Vermont so I can be gay for you
In my mind that wouldn’t be a bad thing to do because my religion is you

I might not go to church
but I believe in a different kind of god than the biblical sort
You can find my god in the steam your breath makes
on the first true day of autumn
or in the idea of a sunny patch
or in the amazing way babies babble
and you understand exactly what they’re saying because
they’re trying to explain what living feels like

You’re so much like poison ivy; keeping me itching
if you were a chef I would be your kitchen
and all we would make would be cheese dishes
because even though you’re a lac-tard
I just want to show you how grate-ful I am
and we can be cheesy together
I’m such a dope around you because I’m addicted and you’re my heroine
Never a villainess but whenever I see you in a dress its villainy
because I have a vendetta against when you wear clothes.
All those soft moans. All these nights I spend alone.
I go through paper after paper as fast as I can so you can tell
how much I pine for you
I could cover you in maple syrup and still only be 39% done
with kissing your body
It’d be sticky but you’d be my pancake and whenever my heart would ache
we could have breakfast in bed

Please remember what you just read, let it fill your head
with the promise of nights that blend into tomorrows
of days filled to the brim
If I could swallow you whole just to carry you around inside of me I would
but we haven’t gone to Jonah’s in awhile
and I’m not whale even though I pretend to be sometimes
So take this as a compliment and shake the fever out
so that I can hold you until my fire dies down
and we can freeze hell over together
It’s okay. We can share my sweater.
