title: before leaving
date: 2024-12
shelf: poetry

A poem is not a promise
and I can tell you’re scared
by the silence you hold in your teeth
the wounds make some madness
and you’re afraid I’ll claim something deep
I am used to those who run
The way to calm a stampede
is to run alongside them until tired
they allow themselves to settle into pasture
Your mind is a stampede
Trying to trample down your own insides
I am not your ghost
I am not here to claim your insides
I do not seek trophies
I am as wild as you are
I am as free now as I ever have been
There is nothing I need from you that I cannot create
Yet
I see goodness in you
joy spreading like fingers
licking rooftops dancing birds
This city is magic and we are witches
let us cast spells on each other
so enchanting are we
drunk on laughter
dancing on each other’s feet
so eager and hopeful
so tender and true
why hate the end before any beginning
because tomorrow begins and ends new
