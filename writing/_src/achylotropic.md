---
no: 39
title: Achylotropic
date: 2015-11
shelf: poetry
book: allograft
---

I want to drive until I see snow
tie my blankets together and climb out the window
catapult into a careening car
headlights blinking just like stars

The branches are daggers cut my teeth on the cold
body so old
mind like a tumor always ready to grow
Radio
broken and stuck
playing Christmas music on repeat
my dumb luck
underneath frozen rivers fish sleep swimming letting the current carry

I get out and forget to lock it
sink my Popsicle toes into the sand
hands in my pockets

I’m Vitamin D deficient
but want to swallow the sun
I hope that the reflection off the moon is sufficient
horizon makes sky and water one
I feel omniscient
winter heart beats like a drum

the entire ocean is frozen
I start to run
on ice
skittering
sliding glass past bare feet
slice
splinter crack fracture
gravity in flight
mirror has broken
water a hand
gripping vice
filling tight
sound mouth suffer smother mother moth candle black night

the sun won’t ever come back
and that’s alright
