no: 101
title: monsoon
date: 2024-12
shelf: poetry
book: litany
---

the rains came and stayed
you think they had other places to be
but they dumped their troubles here
and the basement flooded
so gummed up and full of tomorrow’s jobs
we stood in freezing cold socks
bailing brown water
getting heavier instead of dancing dry

there’s this pressure that builds and builds
right before the eyes
from restless nights
and untamed mind
water flows down down down
until unsettled you can’t help but shake
wringing out sobs
formed into shapes
are you a man or a bag of sand?

it’s been weeks since I’ve written
and weeks since I’ve lied
where does time run off to
when it finally calls it a day

a heartbeat is a white noise machine
my eyes flutter and beat against panes
I miss the heat of a candle
let me curl around it and burn
