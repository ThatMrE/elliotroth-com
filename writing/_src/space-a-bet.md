Every time I see one of those kitchy future-telling animatronics on some godforsaken pier
I always put a coin in
I shake the magic 8 ball and ask a question
I read tarot and trace palm lines to find out
I count down the days before I die
Before I left, I was in this workshop
surrounded by brilliant minds
and we were asked to predict the future
by someone who wants to leave the planet
then when he left the room
nobody had an answer
so I started talking
not caring to look like an idiot
knowing that my words would eventually catch up to my mind to the present
and carry forward the future

They wanted us to make bets
life is a casino and the every day addiction
is taking the coins for Charon
and putting them in a slot
hoping for trip-7s

It’s raining in Costa Rica
and the forecast is for more rain
but there’s eventually a dry season
how do we know these things?
how do you know the sun is going to come back up?
Blame the weatherman
Burn the witch
The prophets are delayed
the world never ends it just changes
who knows who knows who knows

when I ask you
if you want to be in the pictures at my sister’s wedding
it’s not a threat
it’s not a promise
it’s a question about a question
looking at pictures in the future
about what story I want to tell
when someone asks me who is next to me
who wants to be next to me

it rains especially when we don’t want it to
and when we dance it never comes
the prayers remain unanswered
the fortune teller asks for one more dollar
the curse remains in place
and the roulette wheel remains spinning
and I’m counting cards
and watching the rain
wishing you were here
