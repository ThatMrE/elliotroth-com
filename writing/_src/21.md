When I was 20, my former lover’s lover killed himself.
We drink milkshakes every time we talk.
We hardly ever talk about him.
That kind of conversation deserves something stronger.
Whiskey.

When I was 20, my friend’s friend skull cracked.
She fell down a set of stairs and dashed her head
against the corner in front of me.
Art school.
Dark red wine.

When I was 20, my sister’s role model’s heart burst.
All he wanted to do was to heal children.
He didn’t drink.
But he laughed.
Shirley Temple.

When I was 20, my father’s mother was eaten.
Cancer comes slow and steady.
She stole a stein for me from her homeland.
She told me with a wink that no matter what I do, don’t get caught.
Radler.

When I was 20, my cousin’s husband was lost.
His life was art.
He was a humble fighter.
You can see love in his work.
Craft Beer.

When I was 20, the old me died like a garage sale.
With each person that left a little bit of the old disappeared.
An empty room.
An empty barrel.
Moonshine.

So raise a glass.
Toast the unknown and throw it down your throat
and let it echo in your chest.
Drink in your people until you drown and learn how to breathe them.
Drunk and unsteady you find new liquor.
Let it happen.
It’ll be okay.

Life is better with a buzz anyway
