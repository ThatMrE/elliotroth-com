no: 66
title: Ghosts
date: 2015-11
shelf: poetry
book: allograft
---

I sneezed and you said bless you
but I thought you said I miss you
So I asked why

We learned each other’s superstitions
touching roofs running each other’s red lights
kissing knuckles and clicking tongues
to lock pinkie promises tight

I wish people didn’t become ghosts
when they die in each other’s eyes
because I’m tired of people
walking straight through me

Getting ignored is worse than hate
because it means that you’re nothing
not worth the time to
take the effort to answer why

Turns out that I’m just a passing breeze
a wanton freeze-frame in the motion picture
of a VHS B-rate horror flick
an easily forgotten superstition
disappearing into the mist
