no: 88
title: walk the labyrinth
date: 2024-12
shelf: poetry
book: litany
---

I hope you get everything you ever wanted
and I never hear about it
I loved a ghost
she changed her name
and carved away the person
I wanted to build a life with
So i echo as you walk
my heart a cathedral
not caring about the stares

I vomit these words
overdosing on sorrow
finally purging
stumble into the street
climb Bernal one more time

Empty
I start noticing the wind
the grass shifting
Walk the labyrinth
my body an empty church
this is the cemetery
let me put this heart to rest
I walk
tripping over myself
dropping pieces as I go
cutting myself on the shards that remain
my blood a clear white
my tears a deep red
it’s raining now
harder than I’ve seen before
I am here
this place cannot shake me
I am now
my wings unfurl
weak and flimsy
fresh and new
I am ready to see again
I find sea glass at the center
deep ice blue-green
so familiar
