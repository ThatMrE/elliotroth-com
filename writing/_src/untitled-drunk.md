title: Untitled #Drunk
date: 2015-11
shelf: poetry
book: allograft

Huffing fumes
I put on my downstairs shoes
and try not to let my floor suck me down

Holding molten potions of booze
Drunk and confused
Dressed to the best as an American clown

I try to choose
but my brain has fused
So I walk down the block in this strange town

Breathing the blues
I become my own muse
and pretend the end is in the air so I drown
