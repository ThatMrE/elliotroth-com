title: Chick Flick
date: 2015-11
shelf: poetry

As I lay here
in your dark theater
bum knees to chest
as previews of fantasies play
across these sheets
patient
when you’ll be done
editing yourself back together
in the bathroom

like the tiled mosaic
of the sweating linoleum
you’re smooth white feverish
waiting for me to slip and fall

Too late.
I’ve banged my knee
so I’m one short
which only makes me need you more

You are a quilt made of pictures that are stitched together
with hopes and dreams so tightly bound that they blend
into the world’s best romantic comedy.

I want to wrap myself up in you.

You open the door of the bathroom and lay your glasses next to mine.
Frame by frame.
Reel me in with your scent
condense my focus down to 80mm
and I can feel your box office hit
my lips ushers for your hips
So shhhh as my hands slip
she just wants a taste

scars from past lovers can never be erased
