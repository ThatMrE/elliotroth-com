no: 79
title: last meal
date: 2024-12
shelf: poetry
book: litany
---

I want to sip on my ashes with you
I want to dance on our graves
I want to paint frescos in AB negatives
let the cyano smiles dessicate
needless to say I want to commit sacrilege with you
invent religions
then burn down our own churches
pretending to believe in nothing but
really believing in each other
I want to ask you every question
I hate that my sunsets are your sunrise
I love that my sunrise is your sunset
I’m a night owl
I’m sorry I said something about your pigeon noise
there are so many sides to this
and so little time
Do you trust me?
dumb Disney question instead:
would you let the weary ache of our bones form an aqueduct together?
would you kiss a kindness deep into my soul to twist our heartbeats in the wind?
would you steal my memories and make a last sorry meal of them?
I am the murderer
I wish there was a way I could eat your pain
cut my tongue in two
sharp like papercuts
I do not wish to dance on your knife
I will fall on the blade
I will dissemble my desire
picked apart like a sky burial
federated into boxes in the attic
swept under the rug
sleeping on the couch
I slept in the back of a tricycle with a lawnmower engine
flying through a country with 100 languages
hoping to hear yours
I could sleep anywhere
I would sleep on the ground
listen to your twitches above me
feeling the space soften
knowing when it is important
I will make promises and keep them
