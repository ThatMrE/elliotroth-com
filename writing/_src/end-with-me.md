title: end with me
date: 2024-12

There are butterflies in the garden and I am happy
There is a hurricane going on somewhere else but here
it’s the eye of the storm for a bit
and I can sit and sip coffee
and send you memes
and be amazed at being alive
the cabbages are coming in
I’d love to share one with you when you get back
