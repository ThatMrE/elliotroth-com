---
no: 42
title: When you love a woman
date: 2015-11
shelf: poetry
book: allograft
---

understand that you love an echo
understand that you love an iceberg
understand that you love a clock
together you’ll melt, lose time and yell until
your heart will give out
and her shadow will be a blanket
and her breath a religion
and her torso a stanza
to whisper feverishly in the dark
