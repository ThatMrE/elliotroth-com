---
no: 46
title: Little Gone Girl
date: 2015-11
shelf: poetry
book: allograft
---

She was that little gone girl
lost in the last throes of summer
sitting on the second story of an abandoned building
she laughed and jumped
Hit the river hard
waters green, she
sunk down into the muck
came up gasping and naked
trying to hide innocence

We made an oath
that little gone girl and I
traded tattoos, scars and slit wrists
and let our blood mix with ink
to the promise of pretending

We called each other names
she called me old habits
and always fell back into me
I called her sunshine
and basked in her warmth as long as I could
until I got burnt

She was a cherry red iron
branding me with her fingertips
I was a cool drink of water
each word frozen like ice in my throat
breathless, speechless, soundless
we made love in a vacuum
it was cold and airless
so she breathed life into me
and we wrapped our bronze bodies into a furnace
melted into each other
golden children eating the sun
drinking the dust
becoming stars
