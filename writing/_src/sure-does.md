title: sure does
date: 2024-12
shelf: poetry
book: litany
---

do you want to stay up late with me?
some nights when I can’t sleep
I imagine us next to each other trying to tame dragons
most of these fears are mythical things I do to myself
burning fingers soldering
the glow of screens like embers
around the fire
let us hold hands like otters until we fall asleep

I made tea the way you taught me
two bags in a mason jar
do you remember the first night you fell asleep next to me?
like something feral
Love you wild
Love you fierce
Love you until the crows come home

I remember the wonder and joy
when you shook the algae in my closet
each little light dancing
swapping poems about how
you’ve always felt like home

I feel that glow
and when we biked to the top of the mountain
and when I didn’t have enough hands
and when we watched mayhem dig through our trash

wake me in the void
and meet me in the quiet of the morning
the soft steady quiet presence of you
tastes like memory

we can make sweet mouth sounds at each other
hold my pinkie
let’s fall asleep
