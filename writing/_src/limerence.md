Sound is touch at a distance
so let’s read to each other in bed
I hope you don’t think I’m another hypnogogic jerk
I want nothing more than to make nonsense words
until our mouths find better things to do
like licking elbows
our bodies free and wild
cover ourselves in oil
and slide around on the floor pretending to be slugs
entangled, my fingers in your hair
blowing up condom balloons
howling at moons
playing bananagrams to spell out
L-I-M-E-R-E-N-C-E
and other obscure sorrows
I wake up with so many things to tell you
I go to sleep with so many things my body aches to give you
I promise I’ll be home soon

write with me:
I wish you could bite my lip so the taste of your teeth would stay with me
in the echo of my skull the finger of your mind lingers

They say flesh has a memory
So let’s talk it through and the routines that you tongue can trace
You don’t know the games these memories play
Or what id give to have another peice locked away
To come and go when I know you can’t stay

this bed is too big for just one body
let’s dip these weary bones to steep
in the dark we blend like soup skin salty
a moan is a secret promise to keep
