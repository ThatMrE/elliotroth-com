---
no: 83
title: caged bird
date: 2024-12
shelf: poetry
book: litany
---

The day I met you
I was blind to your eyes
deep ice blue-green
so familiar
I don’t think you could see
where my ribcage was cracked
making dumb jokes is a type of bandage
for open heart surgery
pretending laughter is better than sertraline
that’s just to say
I was doing my best to forget
so I cried on the floor of a strange room
in a strange city
full of strange people
whose faces seemed like questions
hanging like hooks around my shoulders
too afraid to go outside
too broke to stay inside
I wished I was a bird in the rain
staring out the window
hungry
and it wouldn’t stop raining
and my wings were cut
I ached like the time I spit out my teeth
my pain a sorry meal
that much easier to whistle the night away
