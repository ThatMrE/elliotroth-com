---
no: 48
title: Carrion
date: 2015-11
shelf: poetry
book: allograft
---

My mouth is dry
My hands are numb
Body dumb head hung
Let the air out of my tires
Deflate mate
You can’t keep running to and
From places until you drop
Dead weight at your feet
Pick me up like a carcass
Nothing but meat
Spit and spin until I’m cooked
Carry on
