My first kiss was a secret shared
whispers and giggles
so young playground playing around
feeling forever in weeks
not knowing how to speak
holding hands on the bus
it was hard enough to make eye contact
a thrown barbie in the basement

My second kiss was discussed at length
a crush caught in the stairwell
feeling the world from lips to cheek
not knowing how to speak
my favorite color her favorite color
de colores se visten los campos en la primavera

My third kiss cut my lip
braces locked split
second before the bell rang
head jerk fireworks
grinned like a fool
feeling the blood run down in a streak
not knowing how to speak
middle school girls are evil incarnate
tortured like a bull in Spain

My fourth kiss I was so nervous
I brought index cards
her spine curved like a question
my body an answer so close
feeling dark us freaks and geeks
not knowing how to speak
she drew a heart on my hand in sharpie
I colored it in every day
writing letters at summer camp
to give to her when I returned
it wasn’t the same

it hurts that I lost count, the thousands of kisses that led me here
so start over
a secret, a discussion, a cut, nervous energy
it all feels new
writing letters over the summer
retracing a name scratched on my body
a name spelled you
