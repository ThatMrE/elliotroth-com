I want this poem to feel like a kiss
to have the taste of plums as a parting gift
I want to feel the space between us
the hollow where your body fit mine
stretch across timezones
I want long trips to not feel like pits
rotting into knots in my stomach
feeling like a strawberry milkshake

Instead
I want to hear the song of your voice
smooth and soft in my ear
the drumbeat of your heart
your pupils dilating
our bodies intertwined
so many things to say while
writing our story together

That’s how I want our goodbyes to be
rituals traditions last rites
building together
just to figure out what it means
on the other side when we say:
"Hi"

It is Yom Kippur
I beat my chest and I am sorry
for all the questions I did not ask
tumbling and left behind like laundry
I am sorry for the imperative clause
I am sorry for an almost perfect weekend
I am sorry for leaving
I guess we needed some angst?

this is just to say
I let the edges of myself melt in our heat
failing to hold that
we do not deserve each other
I don’t deserve a thing
I wanted to shout from my tiger rooftop
drown out dark loud thoughts
so overwhelmed by feeling
my body has been tingling
but I didn’t want to embarrass you
so instead it all feels like a secret
that I don’t ever want to forget

it is brave to remember the good things
my face still hurts from the laughter
another joke for the bucket
not enough hands to hold this feeling
that there’s never enough time from
airport to airport
work and cation
where is the meaning
the balance
goats, horses, dead possum, vulture hawk, wild boar, lizard all a sign
sentience is holy
is this holy? are we holy?
smoke signals kept us inside
mindblowing sex
morning rides like flying
risotto at sunset
climbing trees
acro yoga
climbing each other
showers together
each and every kiss
felt like a solstice to me

I’m still trying to parse my thoughts
they run together like my ink
this is just to say
this matters
you matter
we matter
let’s take our time
