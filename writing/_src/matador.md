---
no: 58
title: Matador
date: 2015-11
shelf: poetry
book: allograft
---

He calls bullshit on himself
sinks a little deeper into his chair
between phone calls home
It’s hard to remember to care
swallow silhouettes in a sketchbook
outlines of dreams
depression is an addiction
and it’s hard to keep clean

Run the bulls on the streets of Pamplona
run them tired, ragged, and hot
run the bulls on the streets of the city
run them until they drop

The pen is a needle
carve twisting veins deep
purple blisters on callused hands
the bruises seem the creep
shut eyes to the world
the world shuts it’s eyes to you
with everyone blind
it’s easy to see truth

head-to-tail, hoof-to-horn
The bulls are roped and led by their balls
eyes-fear-wide, nostrils-flare-rage
The bulls are roped and led into stalls

burrito your sorrow
and mix it with bull’s blood
elixirs taste like sleep
so find other drugs
wave your blankets like favors
to the ghost crowd
a matador’s cape
is but a funeral shroud

oily-mustard trumpets
scream the siren cry
and the mustachioed matador
walks to where he’ll die

Straight ramrod at the center
make yourself taller than you are
when the bulls overtake you
show them where to scar
the crowd shouts for blood
the bulls echoes madness
in Madrid there’s a stake burning for the loser of the contest

he’s magic with the cape
red-fire from his hands
a stumbling-serenade stampede
split-hooves kick up sand

the crowd swells like rippling muscles
teeth gnaw your tongue to rawhide
time waits breathless
for the few remaining strides
hands tremble tension
sword flash, the reflection of eyes
the bull always becomes the matador
so either way you’ll die

Blade drives deep into the heart
Horns into a human sheath
matador and bull as one
relax in eternal sleep
