title: searching for signal
date: 2024-12
shelf: poetry

I only forgot one thing
You
(and a sleeping pad)
oh and a neck pillow
but that’s a sad replacement for
your head against my chest
breathing with your breathing
heart beating with your beating
heart dreaming with your dreaming

I keep forgetting my earbuds
in strange between places
so I have to pay more attention
and can listen close

Sometimes you call yourself a god
and I believe it
so I can whisper across the ocean
and you could hear my prayers

It’s a secret
I want to fall in love with the world
It sometimes pours out of me into the cracks in the pavement where flowers bloom
I want to wake up and shout good morning
I want to drink up the sunrise and kiss you on the eyes and say:
"is this not heaven already?"

put your hand in mine to my heart
plant your daydreams and watch them grow
let us babble on
