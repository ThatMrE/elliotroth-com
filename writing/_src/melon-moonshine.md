title: Melon Moonshine
date: 2015-11
shelf: poetry
book: allograft
---

I’m bushwhacked
weeds choking my heels
and I stumble on the beaten path
dragging along the spoils of war
3 feet of tempered steel
that my Grandpa got off a dead Japanese colonel in Guadalcanal
drawing a line in the dirt with the point and howling at the moon

In my inheritance
there were plenty of hearts to go around
from when he took three shots
and kept fighting
eventually he spent them up to
quadruple bypass mistakes
and drink to make better forgotten memories

I tried to one-up him
I stopped after getting shots five times
because I was feeling light-headed from blood-loss or drinks tossed
I don’t know

but I do know
that I was at that perfect point and time
when stupidity and youth
makes impossible happen

So we found a 19-year-old TV
and beat it to shit
breaking pool cues and hammers
until it threw up glass and electric insides
in our yearly reenactment
of that scene from Office Space
we let ourselves be senseless and violent for brief moments
to make sanity taste sweet like slices of summer watermelon

I took the katana
and clutched it like
a lifeline to shores of former youth
island hopping
crouch low
don’t want to get hit
t-ball stance
fruit ninja this bitch
wait for the pitch
coming right at me
swing slice split straight in half
rotten fruit falls to the side
so I eat the rind
