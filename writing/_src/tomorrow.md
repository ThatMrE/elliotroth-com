Tomorrow I’ll be in mountains
Tomorrow I’ll be another person
probably improbably
the impossible imposition that
takes seven years
to become a new person overnight
Tomorrow it’ll be tomorrow again
and around the edge of cliffs
I’ll see yesterday clinging to the rock face
climbing to the sun

we spend all day going up
to let what ifs pull us down
why not stay low?
the tallest dandelions have their heads chopped off
as per hindu tradition the Maharaja set fire to his widows
Doesn’t that sound like fun?

I’d rather self-immolate than hold
expectations like rats eating out of my hands
marching over my body
unceasing drowning the sky

try to catch me
I am a fever faster than the sun
I am made of small gods
spilling from my belly
I am Shiva destroyer of worlds
I am Krishna cheer the Janmashtami
I am Brahma remade in Bharat
I am Ganesha with my head cut off
Vishnu is dead
in the ice on the mountain
I am tomorrow        I am tomorrow        I am tomorrow
Never today

may I       shiver on false peaks?
may I       dive deeper and risk imploding?
may I       drink from Don Juan pond?
can do     sure does               may be

Tomorrow

we get 3 wishes      and they’re all for more genies
shaken like a magic 8 ball
ask again tomorrow
