She shatters like toy planes
drops like stones
color drains
like melting crayons

Broken glass melodies play
on barefoot dreams
swallow my shadows
taste rusted machines

Drown in gravel
grey swollen riptide
pulls you under
bones for eyes

flay faces
peel me tangerine
rip floorboard smiles
handful of sertraline

we
echo genetic sparks
white-hot thunder pieces
combust fallen apart
