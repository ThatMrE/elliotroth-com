I once made a promise to Poetry that I couldn’t keep
I told her that I’d write her name on my sleeve
but I burned that shirt to keep warm
in an oil-drum fire with some old mementos
that I had kept in a box since 1st grade
I roasted the pictures medium-rare eating memories to keep me alive
until I’m half empty-full

Well take a minute to look at this picture
It’s 3am and we’re sitting in my car
before we decided not to take off our clothes
and you ask me why I never write poems about you as if it is easy
as if these words are not painstakingly ripped from my heart
I give you a half-ass non-committal verbal shrug
that sounds like most of my conversations
with other people

the real reason
is because you are a mirror
I wouldn’t want to stare too long
or risk going blind
staring down infinity
if home is a drain
it makes sense that we keep circling
vultures feeding on each other
picking ourselves apart

You are a crossroads
lost and sold
You are the apple
stare green and gold
You are a mockingbird
cry freedom to the morning
You are a moon dance
pour your liquid body out whole

I am a ghost
a shadow on a silver-lining
I am a riptide
drown beneath my waves
I am a memory
a fallen tree in a forest
I am a poet
I make promises I can’t keep
