title: grab this rope
date: 2024-12
shelf: poetry
book: litany
---

my brain is so noisy
full of a hurricane of wool
molasses in a tornado
circling and tumbling
I’m angry and normally I can throw it
but I’m a mess
and I can’t speak
I want to roar
but my throat is caged
I’m restless today and all the days
i just hide it well
I’m angry and it needs to go somewhere
I bounce it like a ball
so I strain against whatever feels like a prison

I asked you to temper your fear
you decided to put your head inside the lion’s mouth
you don’t have to whip me to tame me
it won’t work
let me be wild in my ways
I’ll let you be feral in yours

I want to roar with you
I want to hunt and kill and feast on the vultures
they caw as if to say carry on
pecking and picking and tearing
away bits of flesh every time
until I’m a ship being plundered of treasure

these stories are all I have
and I hear you call them worthless
while throwing open your chest of war-torn spoils
to stare at me through your kaleidoscope jewels
like fake beer goggles from the past
daring me to walk the plank

please don’t ever ask me to choose
between the horizon and the ground
I’m not digging a grave
I want to be running at something that isn’t a wall
I want to be beyond this
beyond it all
I want you to come with me

I’m not asking, I’m showing
this isn’t loyalty it’s courage
this isn’t a scam
stop treating me like a long con
there isn’t a chance at mutiny if there are no captains
you once told me your thoughts were an ocean
prove it
don’t dash me against the rocks
I’m holding on to you like driftwood
to keep from being pulled away
this is my angry gauntlet and it’s thrown
hand over hand in hand
breathe steady and deep
and trust that we won’t drown each other
