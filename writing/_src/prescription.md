This is a prescription, take it or leave it
like drugs wary of overdose
a few words each day
pacing on the phone
laughter spilling from lips
always moving
You are a flowchart
You are a muse calliope
harmony in our voices
at first
child-like and babbling
falling over each other
sweet like plums given freely
You are so good
you smell of salt and fire
like cool summer storms
your heart thundering
we read each other like maps
comparing scars
tracing outlines of stories yet to be told
we are a collection of maybes
maybe it’s always a maybe
no expectations
no promises
no captains no masters
it will hurt
the best things seem to
do it anyways
