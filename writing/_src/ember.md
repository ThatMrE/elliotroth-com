title: Ember
date: 2015-11

I watched stars being born today.

We were staring up
after the fire had crumbled to embers.
Just you, me
and the stars

There’s a cluster of nebulae out past Sagittarius
that are a nursery
to beacons of light
in the vast cold of space

Did you know
that voyager has that picture
of a Buddhist monk setting himself on fire?
The graphic scene immortalized
for all eternity on the laser-etched disk of memory
sent hurtling at a million miles an hour

It’s a lifeboat of humanity,
an idea to leave a mark
a small coal
black on black

Chances are that it’ll end up at that nebula one day
and dance with a star
its fingers pulling the hope of mankind
down with cold, serious gravity
burning up a painting of
Joan of Arc at the stake
that Buddhist monk
a child covered in Agent Orange
napalm
the books at Krystallnacht
a cross
on the lawn
when our lips touched
kerosene
spark
phoenix               phoenix                                        phoenix
memory
to a supernova flame

We lace fingers around a coal
it burns infinity into our souls

And the stars watch hungry
