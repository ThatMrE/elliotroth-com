title: Rollercoaster
date: 2015-11
shelf: poetry
book: allograft
---

You know how roller coasters pause on the cusp?
They make you think that they’ve crashed or rust-ed
and that you’ll always stay on the brink
Never thinking that it’ll all come crashing down all at once
only to swoop back up again

but for that one moment
you’re terrified
before the ride of your life

Everything slows down
and speeds up
everyone puts their hands up
scream and yell
forget that you have secrets you have yet to tell
and I don’t want to give up
but I think I’m getting motion sickness

I saw a bald eagle with clipped wings
and met a girl with a tan-line ring
which just shows me that it’s not about the things you try
but what you don’t

so I’ll split the night
I’ll weather the fight
I’ll speak in black and white
and dream in color
