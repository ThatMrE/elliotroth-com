---
no: 60
title: Memento
date: 2015-11
shelf: poetry
book: allograft
---

The sands were black
and the dying light caught
struggling in the center of the hourglass
pushing up mountains
Lives lay naked on the shore
Wind whipped bear backs
and I walked

Excuse me miss
but you seemed to have
dropped your eyes
They were heavy-lidded and
fluttered out of your grasp
Are you a dream?
Are you a tourist?
Do you need help?

I am a sight seer
I am a nautilus
I am a golden gate
I am a bridge to your chorus.
I feel like a tourist when I come home
I do not need your help

She wouldn’t take her eyes back
She continued as a leper
dropping pieces of herself
as love letters to faded footsteps
Blind into the ocean
Dissolving
How can you miss someone you’ve never met?
How come there are some you never forget?

I pocketed her eyes as memento

I reached deep in my throat, ripped out my voice
and skipped it ventriloquized against the waves
I swapped skin for sun and lungs for air and hands for water and earth for body
Each nerve shrieking seagull
each finger falling tidepool
I pulled my mind apart and
stopped dreaming

I tore myself apart and followed her
