title: Siren Call
date: 2015-11

Richmond before rain
drives me insane
The city summer sky bends and breaks
and heaven cries for relief
streets deserted
except for the train
that you can hear whistle and moan
like a mourning mother
clear across town
while we chain smoke like guns.

from above
You can see black dots
rushing to hide last minute
as if you could hide yourself from this
wave
slicks the street with bodies
dancing and drowning
becoming a tide of broken homes
the city gasping
while acid eats bones black
our sidewalk covered with
mementos to dissolving dark bodies
Wizard of Oz style
so that when the light hits the water just right
It mixes like blood

I heard gunshots outside my window last night
Staccato cracks in the black
man I wanted to be a man when I moved here but
I’m scared I’ll never reach
Twenty-one homicides in the past
nine months, the city pregnant with rage
mother Richmond raising bullet babies
that eat each other in the womb
When I first moved here
I thought the city was infested with woodpeckers
trying to eat their way out of wood
only to make their own coffins
but I realized I was dead wrong
now I know when I hear death flap its wings
it’s a murder of black birds
with sirens screaming “nevermore”

Jackson Ward is a prison yard
blackmaled into dreaming
you can escape
but there’s a war going on
with 5-to-6-foot something, wearing something, toting something
soldiers trying to take Oregon Hill
18 to 20 something serving 18 to 20 something
while the city Shockoe Slips back into the
Tobacco Row where we were raised
as plantation slaves
and it won’t be long before it all becomes another Hollywood Cemetery with a headstone that reads:
“I once had a dream”

I love playing the game gunshot or firework
on my back porch
it gets harder because we
drink ourselves blind and mute
while celebrating freedom
ignoring the gasps from the gutter
we could see him being
beaten black
and blue
black
and blue
lights paint empty faces
from a parking lot away
because we were too afraid
to say anything
to help a fellow human being
he was beaten by chains he could not break.
uncomfortable
we went inside
but could still hear his animal cries

Those same tears
work like lubricant
for a back-alley score
talking about love like love’s a whore
“she looks like she can take a dick”
“damn look at that ass”
“let me fuck that, baby”

you know nothing about forgiveness
I was beaten
until my lip cracked
and my head bled
red sea split
coughed on my own blood
refused IV drip
metallic and bitter iron

can’t you see my father’s father was once a slave?
chest marked by a bone yellow star

dreads coming down his back
strangling his skull
fist flying
feet flapping
running always running
Exodus

she cried vengeance
over my broken body
but I bled out my anger
forgiveness flows easy
from the side of plenty
all I wanted to know
was why

River city red rain funeral train
Jett Higham, 18 they blew out his brains
a few blocks away
innocent
all he wanted was something sweet from life
so who is to blame?

who is to blame?
