I want to write a poem about how I love you
But we’re out of time
I’ve been saying it to myself
waiting for the right moment
that moment in your kitchen with just the two of us and the tea
In your car zooming back from the airport
the happy cocoon finding each other under the blankets
those times were when we felt like forever
before the alarm went off
I wonder how fast I can say I love you
Can we time it?
Would I be able to get an award or metal?
Fastest I love you in the west
I think in the void
so close
two black holes dissolving in the gravity
of what we were making together
I want to fall asleep next to you whenever and wherever I can
Just so that I can wake up next to you and continue the conversation we were having in my dreams
The blend of it all
You hate when I call it soup
You asked me when I started liking you
And it was too cliche to say immediately
(Also because that’d be bad form)
When I walked home that first night
talking about everything
everything had shifted
And I think you asking me that
Was your way of saying you loved me
And all the other small happy ways
I know
You know
We both know
We love you I us yes
