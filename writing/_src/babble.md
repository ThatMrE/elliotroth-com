I tell myself
just because she writes you poems
she owes you nothing
just because she kissed you
covered in grease in the garage
that can change in an instant
just because you want it to mean something
doesn’t mean that it has to

This tension is like a rubber band
stretching across oceans
bound to break
You tell yourself
building bridges in flood plains
is a recipe for collapse during moonsons

So tame desire
keep the borders of your self solid
do not run like a refugee
from the disaster inside
seeking a safe haven

Long ago you recognized home is made within
not found by hollowing out another
each poem like a salvo in some strange battle
of trying to connect
a tower babbling

There are 1000 languages here and I am trying to understand ours

Are you willing to explore with me?
there are mountains to be climbed
it all is a balance
the weather can change in an instant
I hope these tensions are tamed
my thoughts no longer saboteurs

freedom of movement
freedom of expression
freedom of mind
freedom of heart
Free
