I wish I could write like a man possessed by the spirits of Neruda, Plath, Oliver, Rumi
sitting on this Yucatan ghost
remembering our weekend together
my fingers in your hair
you’re stealing my dreams
a polterheist
it’s fundamental feeled theory
we’re entangled so this is some
spooky action at a distance
I want to haunt you
ouija dance with me?
tiki tipsy mixing red rum
you used your devilish tongue
to read my palms like psalms
make a human sacrifice lighting me up
smoke signals burned our lungs
I’m glad we found a cation to stay inside
well tis the season
what a treat
lay my heart at your feet like a meal
a perfect time to rip out my throat to check for ticks
holding close to listen before I let the zephyr blow through it
I cut off and leave you a hand to hold
I’m sure I can find more
like some kind of Adam’s family reunion
let us snap each other up
naked and shivering in our shadows
warmed by the burning giraffe on your arm
I see this every day on my wall at Agape
walking like a man
Truthfully I am now sur de real
Cuando regrese,
fundamonos el uno en la otra
como una vela quemamos en ambos extrenos
we are clocks
this is an escape room there is only so much time
good thing I have a magnet
and you’ve got steel in your soul
it pulls me to you
the costumes we never planned
the ones we wear now
how well does it fit?
I wish I could rip it all off with my teeth
while you nibble a carrot into a voodoo doll
these snacks are table stakes
fangs to you these thoughts
devour my skull into a brain stew
if you are what you eat then it’s vegetarian
swallow and feel me inside you
I’m trying not to eat so this time goes fast
it might be taboo to think these feelings will last
but for now I’ll stumble in a holy daze
carrion a sacrifice at the altar to a goddess I praise
