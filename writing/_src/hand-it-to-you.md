no: 86
title: hand it to you
date: 2024-12
shelf: poetry
book: litany
---

I don’t know if I’d be good at it but
I want to run my fingers through your hair
past all the worry lines
I could take a sip of joy with you
and pray that this warmth I feel
deep in my chest travels across the space between us
through my rough hands
down your neck
like a drum beat
seeps like tea
into your soul
