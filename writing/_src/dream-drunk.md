title: dream drunk
date: 2024-12

I wish you could bite my lip so the taste of your teeth would stay with me
when you oscillate my metallic sonatas our colorless ideas sleep furiously
I love making your eyes roll as you fight against making noise
a groan, a moan, a laughing tone, I’d stop if I knew you were truly annoyed
with the number of times I’ve been sent to jail I could start a legal revolt
these words sweet reflections of my desire, the joy at what I’ve wrote
I know you hate rhyming poems which is why I use this to tease
break the line for these poems are the best way I know how to show I care
it’d be overly sweet to claim that when our bodies meet it feels like prayer
even in the darkest times we’re rivers intertwined the joy still runs deep
dark blue of a cenote the calm promise of this care is yours to keep
I know you’re a lion, I afraid I might only be a lamb
at least I’m not a fucking goat, I’d hate myself goddamn
For our Halloween costume we could be asleep
I think that might be something each of us need
