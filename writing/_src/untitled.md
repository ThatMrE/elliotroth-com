I’ve been gone 10 days and you’ve already slept with someone
I feel like breaking up with me would have been easier
this kind of murder-suicide feels premeditated
you took the ethical out of non-monogamy
and drove a Camry off a cliff
A self-fulfilling prophecy of being the bronze package
of being unlovable
of not being part of romance
of being alone

You realize this is how my last relationship ended
She didn’t speak out loud
the secrets she was keeping
because she was scared of what she’d find
of what I’d find
so she took the easy way out
and had sex with someone
without knowing what she wanted
without telling me

funny how it was in the same place that it happened
the curse of the east coast
when I’m having a hard time with family
I get reminded that I don’t get one of my own
I was asking you what I should tell my family about you
I was asking if you wanted to fly out to meet them
to see where I grew up
you even took off time during the same weekend
as if taunting me saying "why would I ever want to do that?"
I guess I know what to tell them about you

I asked you to form a community with me
and I thought you said yes
but maybe I misheard
it does get loud sometimes
and I’m noise sensitive
but I thought I was clear
at least with what I wanted
you could have said no

there was a moment when
paused to catch our breath
deep in the jungle
these men a generation removed
talked about how one of them loved
a woman who felt disconnected
she jumped
without realizing how much she was loved
so the moral they told us is to have those hard conversations
tell people what you want
how much you love them

relationship suicide
is when you walk to the edge of a bridge
and jump without a parachute
no preparation
no conversation
no idea of what it means to hold someone’s hand
let alone have them inside you
nobody to catch you on the way down
It’s going to be different now
I’m going to be different now
you can’t have your cake and eat it to
Hi it’s me, cake
happy birthday
I feel stampeded like
a bull in a china shop saw
that I put my heart
in the tea in the mason jar
and left it on the counter for you every morning
and decided to stomp until it was dust
being okay with being dehydrated
with not following flowcharts anymore

Before I left I said goodbye to your room
as if knowing I would never step foot in there again
I slept all day in a room that was not mine
because all I want to do is turn off my brain to stop it screaming
It all feels so temporary
you tipped this metastable state over
and squashed me like a bug
the police will chalk my outline
and they’ll say it was a crime of passion
