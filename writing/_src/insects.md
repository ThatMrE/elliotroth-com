---
no: 64
title: Insects
date: 2015-11
shelf: poetry
book: allograft
---

Spiders on the bridge
weaving webs by the river
as the train tracks click-clack
spiders drain old bodies dry
leaving carcasses out in the sun
to mummify

Heat makes me hazy
and my grip slips
knocking my bottle loose
beneath the rocker
the slick syrupy sweet
trickles around my bare feet
through the cracks in the sidewalk
I watch the ants get stuck
marching one by one
drowning in the midday sun
caramelizing

I have your art supplies still
beneath my windowsill
where crickets come to people-watch
they sing of brighter tomorrows
when lazy sunbeams
catch cobwebs
and ignite
I paint people-watching pictures
on the palms of my hands
like sunscreen

I am a mayfly
only 24 hours until I die
no time for wasting
trap me in a mason jar
and I’ll fight the crystal
beating against glass
until one of us cracks
spiderwebbing fragments
to catch on
until all wound up
we give
and roast in the sun
