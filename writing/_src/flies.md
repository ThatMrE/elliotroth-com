---
no: 52
title: Flies
date: 2015-11
shelf: poetry
book: allograft
---

My girlfriend told me yesterday about a girl
who split her lip
reciting the alphabet backwards
and got her mouth sewn up
and got all drugged up
and slept for days
but when she awoke
her lips were infected
and there were maggots
crawling out of her smile

I once tried
to jump as high as I could
on a moon-bounce
I thought I could flip
I thought I could fly
I was wrong
and kneed myself in the face
and bled for a week straight
and busted my lip
Now every time I kiss
I remind myself
to not reach so high

I don’t understand people
who don’t wear costumes
to costume parties
It’s all apart of the fun
pretending to be someone
you’re not.

Last year I wanted to dress up as a Car Alarm
but people said that
it would be annoying.
All I ever wanted to do
was to make people notice
the burglars
carrying away their battery
but leaving the lights on
and the engine idling

Maybe this year
I’ll dress up as pond scum
and look for someone dressed up as a microscope
so they can make me feel big
Have you ever noticed how
flies love to commit suicide
down your throat
during a
particularly good bike ride?
It’s as if they’re little poets
and they can put wings
to your words
to choke you up
with emotion

I decided not to get stitches
and to let the bump swell
I had to drink water through a straw
and I couldn’t speak so well

So I kept quiet
and even though it hurt

I still shouted in my mind

After a week or so
I woke up to my lip
tingling and itching
dry and cracked
and yawned myself awake
splitting skin and scab
disturbing the hatched maggots
that flew away
carrying poetry
