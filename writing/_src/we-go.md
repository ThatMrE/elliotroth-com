---
no: 41
title: We Go
date: 2015-11
shelf: poetry
book: allograft
---

We skip
class we buy
bus tickets to Richmond we break
into the Interbake we smoke
stacks into the sides of factories we write
promises on walls we white-wash
our war faces we go
we go
we go
we breathe fog
on the river we bless
lady liberty with song we dangle
toes off docks we sweat
with the rhythm of Fridays
You are what you
are what you
eat what you leave
behind we trash
bottles and we break
bones and we love
life as art we are
art we make
love
