title: Widdershins
date: 2015-11
shelf: poetry

You know that magic trick?
Where there’s two rings
or more
and the magician strings them together
into chains
with a wave of his wand?

Abracadabra

I see magic everywhere
friends become disappearing acts
now you see them
now you don’t
notice the black rings under their eyes
that’s the key to this trick

The most magical time of night
is 4 am leaning over a bridge
levitating above the road
caught in the circle of light
of a streetlamp
like a moth
waiting for it to flicker out
I like watching the circles of headlights pass by
wishing I was brave enough
to swallow a flame

I have friends who are fire-eaters
but most just like blowing smoke rings
and laugh because I can’t
even though I always try
A burning circle of coal
lit our faces that night
as we talked about how we always come back
to the same people
over and over
because there’s no tricks to them
and that’s the real magic

When we had enough
of poking holes into our souls
and clouding our minds with smoke
so that our thoughts choked up
and our words flowed freely
I walked her home
and tried to blow smoke rings
into the cold air
I kissed her goodnight
and tasted ashes
on her tongue ring
as my breath and hers
twisted into infinity

Let me let you in on a secret:
the key to the trick is a small gap
held hidden in the magician’s hand
if you aren’t distracted
you can see how he flits back and forth
between bound and free

Magic.
