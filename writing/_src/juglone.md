no: 54
title: Juglone
date: 2015-11
shelf: poetry
book: allograft
---

Listen to the drone of the keyboards and the scratch of pens
There isn’t much diversity in ecology
It’s the North Face effect
the brand makes the person
so get your iPhone out to Snapchat pictures
instead of paying attention when the teacher talks about extinction

We learned about competition and we
learned about parasitism
There seem to be a lot of things in common
with how we copy from each other’s tests
to move up the bell curve
to survive

As you grow bigger, your population density gets smaller
you get lonelier
The popularity of leaders is measured
in the number of people who want to kill them

my favorite term for plants is self-pruning
They cut off what is not needed
Juglone drips from the walnut to kill those who lie below
while the floor of the forest decays
hear the rustle of dead-tree paper in the classroom

try to survive
