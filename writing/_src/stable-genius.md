You write a diss track as if that’s an explanation
When truth is there’s a lot of me that you can’t face and
conversations unfinished dangling clauses like NORAD
prevent the pilot dropping gaslight violence don’t tell me my plans

It’s hard enough in here without also managing your ego
Tell you what I’ll shut up if you stop comparing me to Nero
Hedonic supersonic treadmill I’m not that kind of people
Do my best to raise you up when I’m feeling like a zero

I want to build a world that’s been caught up in my imagination
A fucking dinosaur I’m the king feel my roar of frustration
involved not impressed give you some words of affirmation
My queen, it’s not a secret here’s the steps to domination:

Step 1: get a gun to shoot holes in the control of the population
understand you have power when you change the means of production
Step 2: tell the truth loudly and in front of all the stations
share in the care of people to form your own nation
Step 3: leave them be the ones that don’t join the revolution
pretty soon we’re worm food so they don’t deserve your salvation
Step 4: be a force that is in constant creation
mutate never abicate focus without cessation

You may think I’m a menace or trumped up in spirit
but every day I don’t write I feel I’ll die before I see it
this world that I’m shaping God complex call me Jesus
double cross at what cost
