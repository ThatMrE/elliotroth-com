no: 77
title: love to the point of invention
date: 2024-12
shelf: poetry
book: litany
---

I wish I didn’t remember my jacket
On the back of your kitchen chair
So you’d have something
that smelled of me
because saying words like
I like the shape of your thoughts
the sweet wickedness of your tongue
on full display
and how they jig and jagsaw
cutting through the haze of Delhi
each word a fruit bursting
ripe and juicy and filled with seeds
planted deep in my brain
is not enough
it’s not enough to invent a language
to play hide and seek
to lose at staring contests
to remember the warmth on the mountain
sitting under old trees who have seen this before
to hear the whoosh of a heartbeat in your ears
the calm knowing
that there is a space that sits
between the swoop of your neck
and your back
that fits my lips
the softness of your ocean eyes
I know I am a memory
I know I am a poet
I know I am true
I dare us to lie
down down deep
miles away
your echo a shadow next to me
whisper me to sleep
