---
no: 38
title: Peanut Butter and Bananas
date: 2015-11
shelf: poetry
book: allograft
---

I broke down crying
making peanut butter banana slices
and grilled cheese
cut in triangles
because no matter how well I made it
it would never taste the same
as the nights I would pad downstairs
from a nightmare
for a midnight snack
and my mom would pour me a glass of milk
and talk troubles away

Now
sitting on the floor of my kitchen
nose running
I wonder if she ever thought her peanut butter banana slices weren’t good enough
they weren’t close enough to how her mom made them
they only tasted like regret
with a dollop of nostalgia
and she ended up on the floor of her kitchen
after I padded back upstairs
