fall is the season for apple picking
and apple was my first word
I said it as an interruption
when my family was making sauce
because I wanted to be included

it’s bittersweet how belonging
isn’t enough to just be
it has to have longing tacked on
and maybe I’ll run out of clothes for you to borrow
and maybe these aren’t the right words
and maybe I’m not fast enough but
I go slow because I want to savor this
savor us

I’m so tired that
I’ve been failing to dream
nightmares are for people with things to lose
teeth, trophies, clothes and minds
I want to know what scares you

Here’s what scares me:
tasting the metal of you hitting your head
like smashing pumpkins
the day after Halloween
fear is the mind murder
this an imposition
like dancing on motorcycles
this damn thing is cursed

I don’t want fears to shape
the space between our bodies
especially when
my mind fails me
with memories like quicksand
sometimes I’m drowning
sometimes I remember to breathe

you help me remember
looking back I always assume
these are the best days
even at their worst

And sometimes I want you to feel the scratchiness of my beard
because it’s only that way for a short time
And sometimes I want to feel hurt
so I know how good things really are
And sometimes
breathing in the smell of head and shoulders two-for-one
I thank how holy it is
every morning a sunrise
these happy few minutes drowning together
breathing in your sand
before waking up
