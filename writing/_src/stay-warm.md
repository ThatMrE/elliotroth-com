title: stay warm
date: 2024-12
shelf: poetry
book: litany
---

Never boring I
curl your words like a whisper around
my body rain outside awash in
daydreams without worries I
wrap myself in you and
feel the tension pulling loose while we
grow together as if
rooting for each other we
figured out that
the mycorrhizal sparks from my fingers in your hair
my legs twisting around yours
are the only saving grace
from the cold chill in the air
