title: turning over four leaves
date: 2024-12

My favorite time of year is blackberry season when everywhere feels like being outside
When I can walk and eat until I’m stained
Whenever I can I look for four leaf clovers
Maybe because I believe I’m unlucky
Maybe because I want to engineer some luck
To plan and predict and make my own
Genetically ensure that there will be fields of luck
Spread out as far as the eyes can see
That it’s preordained
as opposed to crawling on my hands and knees through the grass
Looking for something rare

On the longest day of the year my mind feels stretched like a steamroller
squeezed like juice and wrung out
a series of ducks on a line
these are the kind of days where waiting takes forever
so don’t wait anymore
the day is old
and the retirement community hasn’t served the midnight jello

Why do people ever take down Christmas lights
Why don’t spiders have wings
Why is life measured by laundry
the chime about to go off
It’s now my favorite time of night
1234
Count sheep
in a field of clover
