---
no: 36
title: Christmas Day Meal
date: 2015-11
shelf: poetry
book: allograft
---

Slicing beats
sweet hands
slightly sugared white snow
fingers peeling pomegranates
ice cold water split open
cut palm don’t notice
red
down garbage disposal
bleeding
stains on hands and clothes
from the meal
cherry tomatoes pop and gush
juice dribbles down chin
blood orange pulp
purple carrots crunch
dark corn shine
sagging roasted red peppers
bitter bone-white radishes
ruby on the inside
wilted spinach and guacamole
a true Christmas
macabre meal
single rose centerpiece
knife flashes
massacred plates
lips dried and cracked
bag it up
wash stains off hands
clean remnants away
down garbage disposal

for dessert:
red velvet cake marbled
like raw meat
cherry icing
bowls stained off-pink
