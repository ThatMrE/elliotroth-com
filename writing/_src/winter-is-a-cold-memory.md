---
no: 50
title: winter is a cold memory
date: 2015-11
shelf: poetry
book: allograft
---

The moon was a toenail
the day we left
and I wanted to give myself hiccups on that cold January night
watching as
she kissed him deep
in the parking lot of the metro
and then waltzed back home
shins skyscraped
from a city boy’s stubble

I reminisced about what we never made
hands in my pockets
thinking that
my friends are like a box
full of mismatched gloves
sputtering
gently we run
batteries on empty
writing in each others yearbooks
then passing it on

My breath mingled with your smokestacks
as we drove
while the air between us was empty
between each rattling toke

They have me taking sun supplements now
because when you bury yourself
starving
nostalgia is a needle infusion
of pre-chewed sunlight

and so we sat in silence
everything had already been said

She asked why I was quiet
and I answered:
“because it is loud in my head”
