title: Uncertain

It was when we were driving back from Florida
mostly lost
that I asked you why there wasn’t a better way to describe uncertainty
Not in the sense of standing on the brink looking into the void
being at the precipice of unknown
but more in the murky sense of searching
you were quiet for a bit then said:
“It’s like searching for objects in a glass of milk”
or maybe it’s like swimming in milk
grasping at any kind of cheerios life raft you can find
even if some are broken
or some don’t fit on your spoon
“It’s like starting a story in the middle” I answered
where the ending isn’t written
and you don’t know the beginning well enough
to figure out where you are

being uncertain is like prison inmates exercising before death row sentencing
or pretending to be the person you have to be

I am in the void, off of the edge, already squinting in the black
searching for signal in noise

We kept driving
passing millions of strangers
still uncertain of where we were
