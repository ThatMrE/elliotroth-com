So I’ve had this bad case of regret-fueled nostalgia
It’s like insomnia but with a badder bite and it keeps me up at nights
After I get sick with alcohol and turn to you
Your number is blazed into my phone in telephonic code
and the later it gets the less I text until
Hey girl what are you up to? You free? DTF?
Until it’s finally just a solitary question mark
with its back bent with the weight of secrets
spine twisted with scoliosis of the soul
head raised to the sky in a barbaric yawp at the full moon
We’re both loony and I can’t control myself

they say that Misery loves company and Loneliness is her best friend
they were down for a threesome but I ended up with Cowardice at the end
And what’s funny is no matter how much I try to pretend
I still knew she had a boyfriend

I belong in an asylum
because every touch get is electric shock therapy that reinforces
the deadening of emotion
I’m trapped in the padded-walled cell of my mind’s commotion
and the only solution is this vicious cycle of meaningless sex
If it was meaningless why do I always want to make her breakfast the day after
If it was meaningless why do we always dissolve into laughter
It’s that laugh attack that feels like a heart attack
and although I’m an EMT I was never trained for this
I die each time and she always brings be back with mouth-to-mouth
Doesn’t matter that my first sexual encounter
was with a girl that wouldn’t even kiss me
doesn’t matter that after she was done she didn’t even miss me
because brailed lies imprinted on thighs, sighs, oh my, tongue tips touch lips
My carefully constructed marionette of a body pulled along by her fingertips
all it takes is her soft breath to bring me crumbling down. What now?

pretend.

pretend like she don’t matter
like you’re ticking off the days ‘til the end of the month
like every time I see her she doesn’t disassemble my insides
And wrench my guts up and tie me like they’re fuzzy cuffs
I don’t even know if I’m into that stuff
I wonder if she can see through my act
This laser-eyed beauty drawing and quartering me
Uncurling what we’ve agreed can’t happen
Demanding a confession of how lost I am
In the desert wasteland
Of your Mona Lisa smile
