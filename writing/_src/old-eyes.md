no: 96
title: old eyes
date: 2024-12
shelf: poetry
book: litany
---

I want my eyesight to get worse
so we can share glasses
the blurry shapes of each other blending
like coffee and tea
your fast breath and my fast heartbeat
making for slow mornings
you told me that you want me to be as restless as you
I’ve been testing it out but I’ve got another flavor
rather than anger
I’m trying for fierce joy
the kind that bites your lip and the taste of teeth
the kind that paces like a tiger high above
the kind that flies down pavement arms outstretched hugging the world come what may
let me leap out of bed only to return to you
let me flee my house only to find home
let us dance and play and make and sing
and on quiet days
I will ask you to sing to me
and I hope you will ask for a bedtime story
