title: wound up
date: 2024-12

Let the soft human of my body go
Taste the start of fall on my breath
Walk the labyrinth
Test the flowers of their patience
These commandments on the mountain
To get lost; to echo truth
To make big promises and keep them
To separate the narrow path from the range
Climbing mountains never feels right until the end
Pain is a real thing
Like ashes in your mouth
Reminder that you burn bright
And there is only so much room around your fire

I sometimes wake up afraid that my mouth is sewn shut
that these words aren’t enough
that these bones will become dust and I
wish that I was bigger than the person that I am
I bury myself in sheets and burn down all my plans and I
wonder if my parents felt the same way
If they pretended that the dreams they held were shit they couldn’t say and I
never had a broken bone so you can’t say that I’ve healed
I’ve never been told to be quiet trying to shout the things I feel and I
wouldn’t wish silence on my worst enemy
these words pile up like a car wreck deep inside of me and I
love being around you because it feels like you punch a hole through the paper bag soul of me
hold me letting it flow out like it’s okay it’s okay it’s okay
we bleed and it’s okay these things like stab wounds
let them run until they’re dry
In EMT training they taught me you never pull out a knife
you leave it in and I remember thinking: does that mean for life?
what happens after we drop them off at the ER isn’t up to me but letting go is the hardest thing
it’s like unwrapping a bandage around this knife and pulling it out of me
and I’m afraid I’ll never stop bleeding
and I’m afraid that I won’t have any more life to give
and that if I do it I’ll die
that these words will flow and I’ll die on the pavement
and they’ll say: he shouldn’t have taken the knife out
