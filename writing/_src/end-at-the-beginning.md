title: end at the beginning
date: 2024-12
shelf: poetry
book: litany

shhhh
it is wrong to express your joy so loudly
this mind is a library
walk softly and carry your baggage
don’t drop it too quick on my toes
I’m tired
I’m tired of being tired
it’s a ride and I want to get off
all of the guts are spilling out
all at once
I’m embarrassed
I’m embarrassed of being embarrassed
to say vulnerability is like
running naked backwards through a cornfield
hoping you don’t trip and fall

when you get cut
or bit by a rattlesnack
they tell you to suck the venom out
then spit as hard as you can
before the pain curdles in your mouth

can’t you see I’m trying
but people don’t see the try
flipping to the back of the book
you see the ending first
to figure out if it’s good to read

this is just to say
when you’re thinking about endings
it’s hard to enjoy the taste of sunshine
dribbling down your chin
your tremble and tremor feels like an earthquake
may you jump and never find ground
headspun with wings aplenty
kiss the light
run forward into my widespread arms
whatever may come
