title: Harvest Moon
date: 2015-11

It’s that time again
When a backhanded goodbye is not enough
and you’re looking for a full-frontal face fuck
because the pregnant moon won’t stop bitching and moaning
how it has needs
and all you need is a good fight

so you go out looking
tomcat brawls in back alley dens
where the concrete is as hard as you
and with the metallic taste of blood from biting your own tongue

You feel like you can build a statue out of your own iron

Kick a can
Kill a man
Punch a wall

Bruises are topographical maps showing how high you have climbed
(and how low you can go)

so plant a blood orange
let it choke your garden out like auto-erotic asphyxiation
water it with blood and tears
let it grow like a man
Cut it down in fall
Reap what you have sown
grasp it from its roots and pull until the sky screams
Sickle-celled tang between your fists
squeeze until the juice runs dry
dribbles down your chin

get ready to starve this winter
