What do you do when you can see someone else about to be lonely?
I am but a body holding a place in line
I am but a shattered mirror I am what bored people do on a Saturday afternoon
I have hands that can fill spaces but I don’t have enough hands
I can hear your panting need
I can see where I used to shape the clay of myself to fit the gaps
you can see the light of self shining through
I am useful I am used and please fill my
mouth with the taste of danger
to fall into this again is electric
I throb and moan and bite my tongue
put something inside me and give me something to bite down on
But no -
this anticipation is delicious
actually -
even more now than ever
there is a clear wall where my shadow lies
I am tied and gagged and bound
but I’ve done the knots myself
I am no longer begging
to fill my bowl with you
I am more than a snack I am a meal
I can say no
I can say later
I can… this is just to say
we are different
we are you and I
separate
on a hot day the last thing I want is soup
I trust me to trust myself
stop thrusting myself
instead knowing I am someone else
I want to take my time
It is easy to drown the self in the roar and rush of sweat
It is hard and special to know
to deeply know
to know more than anything
that I am whole and complete
I am irreplaceable
I am not with or without desire
I am enough I am enough I am enough
