no: 102
title: noisy season
date: 2024-12
shelf: poetry
book: litany
---

After every conference I worry I’m dying
that the people I love are dying
That newtons first law applies
and that if I stop moving I’ll never start again
That the next year I won’t be the same
I won’t see the same people
That I’ll be too tired
or they will have moved on
and that the brief interludes when we harmonize
will unsync into noise
That the echo of their voices will stop reverberating my ear
That I’ll forget and be forgotten
that the only time life feels like an episode of friends
when I can gather my people around me like a blanket
is when we are worried about jobs and money and unimportant things
because we’re about to be cast out like seeds into wind
sown across the world and I’ll be alone trying to dig deep roots with nobody to lean on
I’ll topple so I shake because it’s all a storm
coming on quick then gone
where do they all go when you find home in other people
it all feels like front doors slamming
and another early morning
get up, get into a borrowed car and drive
