isolation is the gift
I masturbate until it doesn’t help anymore
I write until it doesn’t help anymore
I eat until it doesn’t help anymore
I stay up late bingeing shows until it doesn’t help anymore
insomniac lobotomized nostalgia
Amazing how much you can’t get done when you don’t want to think

Instead, I sleep with strange dreams of Armageddon
I do not belong here
I walk until my feet blister
I do not belong here
End to end to end to end
singing soft songs to myself of broken wings
I do not belong here
I’m given a bracelet that says:
"it’s all about how you dance in the rain"
so you climb Bernal every day in bad weather
and walk the labyrinth to clear the mind
at least it isn’t LA

My grandparents used to walk the beaches together every morning
like I used to walk the beaches down south
picking up sea glass
hundreds of pounds of the stuff
A testament of their love, of time
we still don’t know what to do with it
it sits in trash bags in storage

I cleared out my storage unit
everything down to a backpack
a suitcase
burned the rest
my broken bike sold for parts
I am but pieces of a man
leaving quietly in the night
