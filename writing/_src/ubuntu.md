saying I miss you
isn’t enough to convey
the depth of feeling
the phantom limb ache
like an octopus
love is a distributed nervous system
stretched over the world
Stone shaped into a church
my spirit a religion built of bodies
caverns carved echo chambers into cathedrals
Where you used to be

When you walk alone in the morning sunrise of Vienna
or dance on the cobblestones of old Delhi
tears running down your face from the years
I feel the space of your body
the holiness within my own
my heart no longer a drum beat
but a harmony singing in the altar
we are, we are, we are

I am obsessed with words that do not exist
untranslatable things that we are
our true names never passing lips
the hidden desire to know and be known
at the beginning and end
we circle and share the stories
the emotional resonance of a sunrise
Life is meant to be shared
a broken promise
no matter how alone it tastes
You are part of me
always
